A dense newspaper-style image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 — 19 stories on one page: a navy masthead with the date, a "THE DAY IN UTC" timeline, three illustrated lead stories, eight cards, a numbered briefs strip, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" map and a "DATE CHECK" footer.

Leads: Xi greets Trump on the tarmac in Shenzhen as APEC opens; Kuwait and Bahrain intercept Iranian drones after the US strike on Qeshm Island; Voyager 1 crosses one light-day from Earth. Cards: Russia fires 141 drones at Ukraine (126 downed); the EU proposes a €45bn loan for Kyiv in 2027; Fed minutes point to more hikes; Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast; Amnesty reports 37 killed by RSF drones in El Obeid; Hakeem Jeffries wins the Democrats' Speaker nomination 251–11; GTA VI goes on sale after a 13-year wait; Ebola passes 11,000 cases in DR Congo. Briefs: Peltola wins Alaska's Senate race (56–44); Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge for a second day; Ukraine strikes the Ilysky and Afipsky refineries; the US–China tariff truce runs to 10 January 2027.

v2 redesign (19 Nov 2026): rebuilt in the dense front-page style the user asked for — timeline strip, detailed flat illustrations per story, numbered briefs, world map and date check — using only verified facts. The image is drawn as a front page and the program copies it to the output directory unchanged.

v3 (19 Nov 2026): briefs strip expanded from 4 to 8 briefs (12–19) with four verified additions; illustration detail enriched across the three leads and all eight cards; counts updated to 19 stories throughout.

Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. The image is 1024×1024 PNG.
